An ecological ceiling is a upper thershold or limit of the Earth's systems that we must not exceed to ensure a safe operating space for humanity and subsequently businesses

The planetary boundaries represent the nine planetary processes that are scientific community has identified as being essential to maitnaining a stable and resilient Earth system: climate change, biosphere integrity, land system change, freshwater change, biogeochemical flow, ocean acidification, atmospheric aerosol loading, stratospheric ozone deplation, and novel entities.

8. Stratospheric ozone depletion

The stratospheric ozone layer protects life on Earth from harmful ultraviolet radiation. The thinning of the ozone layer in the upper atmosphere, primarily due to human-made chemicals, allows more harmful UV radiation to reach Earth's surface. The current total amount of stratospheric ozone is within safe levels, and recovery is ongoing, with values still below mid-20th century levels.

Ozone depletion is significant in the following ways:
- Ozone blocks dangerous UV rays.
- Scientists detected a major thinning in the Ozone layer in the 1980s, caused by common chemicals and appliances (e.g., CFCs, a chemical that was used in aerosol cans, fridges, air conditioners).
- In 1987, every country in the world signed the Montreal Protocol to phase out targeted substances.
- In the following decades, ozone levels were restored back to the safe operating space.

Ozone recovery offers a reason for hope as it illustrated our ability to take collective global action.
