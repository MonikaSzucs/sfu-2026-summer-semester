# Issue Memo - Individual ASsignment

Details
For this assignment, you will prepare one of the appendices that will be included as part of the team lifecycle project. Your team will be assigned a simple product for the team project. Each person within a team must select one of the following broad issue categories: (a) climate change, (b) water, (c) ecosystems/biosphere, (d) pollutants and waste, (e) worker rights and wellbeing, or (f) community rights & resilience.*

*Each member of your team must focus on a different issue/topic category. It is up to you as a team to decide what issues to focus on. 

A detailed breakdown of issues that is very helpful can be found here: https://www.embeddingproject.org/resources/scan-a-comprehensive-list-of-sustainability-issues-for-companies 

[List of sustainability issues for companies](https://embeddingproject.org/resources/scan-a-comprehensive-list-of-sustainability-issues-for-companies/)

This assignment has two components:

1. You need to undertake background research on the issue for which you are responsible and outline the following in a written document:

Broad/general questions about your issue
What is your issue category (explain it) and why does it matter?
What are some important trends in society related to this issue category?
Specific questions related to your issue and product
What does the lifecycle look like when viewed from the perspective of this issue category? You must sketch it out and map out the impacts according to your issue, and then explain it sufficiently. A standalone visual doesn't speak for itself.
Where should an organization prioritize its efforts to make improvements related to your issue and why?
What are be some interventions (e.g., solutions) that you could take to address the identified areas of prioritization?
2. You need to record a video of yourself answering the following question:

What surprised you the most when completing your issue memo and why? Explain your answer.
Format
You will hand in a written document that will eventually be added to your team report. Think of this as preliminary research and a summary of your findings. This document will be part of your appendices to support your final team report. Select a referencing system and employ it consistently. 

You will also hand in a video recording (not shared with your team or classmates). Think of this as a bit of a personal reflection on your issue memo.

Please hand in your assignment as a PDF document and video recording. It should be suitable for a professional environment.

> `3 pages (maximum) and 2-minute recording`
