## Doughnut Rconomics and Ecological Ceiling

Humanity's survivel  depends on a health planet. A cruicial aspect of business ustainability is understanding that ther eis an ecological ceiling consisting of the planetary boundaries.

An ecological ceiling is a upper thershold or limit of the Earth's systems that we must not exceed to ensure a safe operating space for humanity and subsequently businesses

The planetary boundaries represent the nine planetary processes that are scientific community has identified as being essential to maitnaining a stable and resilient Earth system: climate change, biosphere integrity, land system change, freshwater change, biogeochemical flow, ocean acidification, atmospheric aerosol loading, stratospheric ozone deplation, and novel entities. 

![](/BUS716-sustainability/module-3/img/model_of_doughnut_evonomics.png)

"crossing boundaries increases the risk of generating large-scale abrupt or irresversible environmental changes. Drastic changes will not necessarily happen overnight, but together teh boundaries mark a critical thershold for increasing risk to people and the ecosystems we are part of. Planetary boundaries are interdependent, meaning that if we cross one boundary, we will affect others, or even cause them to cross out of the sage operating space." stockholm resilience centre

In identifying the planetary boundaries, the scientific community asked themselves. "What are the sage operating limits of our planet, and what changes can we force on it before we trigger rapid, catastropgic environmental harm?

some of these are considered core boundaries. If any core boundaries alone is exceeded, it could significantly impact and change the trajectory of the Earth's systems. Climate change and biosphere integrity are considered core boundaries. Some experts have started to also consider novel entitites as a core boundary.

### Seven planetary boundaries now exceeded
six of the nine planetary boundaries (climate change, biosphere integrity, land system change, freshwater change, biogeochemical flows, and novel entitites) were exceeded by the beginning of 2025. A seventh planetary boundary - ocean acidification - was clsoe to its critical threshold and then exceeded later in 2025. Thsi updated figure is reflected throughout its module.
