# Module 3: Platform Architecture & Ecosystem Design

## Topic 
- Designing the core interaction: pull, facilitate, match — the three levers every platform must get right
- Openness decisions: who can build on you, who can't, and what happens when you get the dial wrong 
- How new ventures can plug into existing platforms — Shopify, AWS, App Store — instead of building from scratch 
- UGC and social media as platform architecture sub-topics: how user-generated content drives flywheel effects 
- Ecosystem roles and the risks of depending on a platform you don't control (first look at dependency — deepened in S7) 

## Takeaway
Core interaction blueprint — participant flow diagram showing who arrives, what they exchange, where you capture value
