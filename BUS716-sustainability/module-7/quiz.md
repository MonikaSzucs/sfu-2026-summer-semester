# Quiz

1. In the Network for Business Sustainability resources, Fairphone's approach to sustainable innovation primarily focuses on which of the following? (Select the best answer)
- Changing production processes to use recycled materials, fair wages, and modular design

2. According to the Sustainable Business Model Canvas framework, why is it important for businesses to identify their negative externalities? (Select the best answer)
- So they can address them proactively and avoid accusations of greenwashing

3. A car manufacturer decides to reduce energy consumption in its existing production facilities for its current vehicle models without changing the fundamental design or features of the cars. This approach best exemplifies which category of sustainable innovation outlined in the Network for Business Sustainability resources? (Select the best answer)
- Operational optimization

4. Based on the Network for Business Sustainability resources, sustainable innovation involves making intentional changes to a company'sproducts, services, or processes to achieve which combination of outcomes? (Select the best answer)
- Long-term social and environmental benefits while creating economic profits

5. In the the Network for Business Sustainability resources, the coffee capsule machine example illustrates which key problem that sustainable innovation seeks to address? (Select the best answer)
- Successful products that create unintended negative environmental consequences

6. According to the Network for Business Sustainability resources, the "systems building" approach to sustainable innovation is characterized by which of the following? (Select the best answer)
- Collaborating with others including partners from unrelated industries and marginalized actors

7. Which of the following key messages does the article on innovations in India illustrate about technological innovation and societal change? (Select the best answer)
- Technological innovations can bring benefits while simultaneously creating negative social consequences

8. The world is full of examples of sustainable innovations. According to the Financial Times video on sustainability ideas, what is the primary challenge with making buildings more sustainable? (Select the best answer)
- Old buildings represent 80-90% of building usage, making them the key target for sustainability improvements

9. According to the Network for Business Sustainability resources, why don't more firms engage in sustainable innovation despite its benefits? (Select the best answer)
- It requires time, focus, and effort, making it challenging to implement

10. The three categories of sustainable innovation outlined in the Network for Business Sustainability resources, representing a continuum from least to most impactful change, are which of the following? (Select the best answer)
- Operational optimization, Organizational transformation, Systems building
