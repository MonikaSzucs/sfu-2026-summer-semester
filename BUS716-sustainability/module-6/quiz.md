1. According to the Network for Business Sustainability (NBS) resources, recycling is an important part of the circular economy, but it can be less desirable than reusing and repairing because of which the following reasons? (Select the best answer)
- It uses additional energy and resources, which can take away some of value

2. According to the Embedding Project, when it comes to sustainable procurement, it is easy to underestimate ____________________ (Select the best answer)
- The power of asking questions to suppliers

3. How did Patagonia first become aware of migrant worker recruitment fee issues in its supply chain? (Select the best answer)
- Social responsibility audits conducted in 2011 revealed red flags, which Patagonia then investigated in depth with the NGO Verité

Which of the following waste issues are addressed by the businesses featured in the documentary? (Select all that apply) - No partial credit, must select all correct options to earn a point.
- Food waste

5. In the case of Patagonia, what structural feature of the labour contract cycle made the debt burden on migrant workers particularly severe? (Select the best answer)
- Workers could take up to two years to repay a labour broker, and most contracts lasted only three years before they had to return home and begin the fee cycle again

6. When making procurement decisions, the Embedding Project recommends that you make sure they _____________________ (Select the best answer)
- Are informed by your sustainability goals and priorities

7. According to the Network for Business Sustainability (NBS) resources, which of the following are key challenges for the circular economy? (Select all that apply) - Partial credit awarded
- The circular economy is complex as it often depends on good communications, material availability, and collaboration with unlikely partners
- The circular economy requires consumers to embrace key behaviours such as reducing purchases, reusing products, and recycling

8. When it comes to sustainable procurement, the Embedding Project argues that the key question that you need to ask when assessing the impact of a product is ____________ (Select the best answer)

9. When Patagonia discovered that suppliers in its Taiwanese supply chain had charged migrant workers unlawful recruitment fees, what did it do? (Select the best answer)
- It stayed in the supplier relationships, required reimbursement of past fees, set a deadline to eliminate future fees, and continued commissioning audits at its own expense

10. In the case of Patagonia, what regulation/legislation led Patagonia to disclose their discovery in their supply chain? (Select the best answer)
