Message from Your Instructor
Hello and welcome to "Sustainability!"

I am thrilled to be your instructor for this course offered by SFU's Beedie School of Business.

Tackling grand societal challenges have become an urgent imperative, and it's safe to say that businesses have a key role to play in supporting the development of a sustainable society and future. In this course, we examine the relationships between business, society, and the planet, and what it means to be a responsible business and how businesses can do their part to be a catalyst for system-level change. 

With the course exploring pressing societal challenges such as climate change and inequality, at any given point in this class, you and/or your classmates might find yourself uncomfortable or at ease given the current state of a specific challenge and/or experiences with one. Please know that if you ever have any questions or concerns related to this course, do not hesitate to reach out to me. I am here to support you in this course.

This course has been designed to provide you with useful knowledge and skills that you can embrace as professionals, managers, team members, and leaders to contribute to businesses. There is so much to cover when it comes to business and sustainability and I wish we could cover it all. However, I've done my best to design and build out a highly structured and engaging course for you on this important topic that I am passionate about and have dedicated the better part of my life to. My hope is to prepare you for the future of business and society.

I am fully committed to doing what I can to make this course a success for you. This is your course and you are all important contributing members of the community. I want nothing else for you to have a positive and generative experience as this course progresses, so if you have questions along the way, please do raise them directly with me.

Note: Your "Student Profiles" are extremely helpful. If you haven't completed yours yet, please do. They provide helpful information that let me better tailor the course to you.

When it comes to contacting me about an inquiry or setting up a meeting, the best way to reach me is by email.

I look forward to navigating this course together with you and your classmates.

- Kam
