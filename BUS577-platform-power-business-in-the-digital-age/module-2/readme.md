# Module 2: Network Effects — the Engine of Compounding Value

## Topic 
Direct vs. indirect network effects — which do you actually have, and how strong are they?
Distinguishing network effects from virality, brand effects, and scale economies — the dot-com trap
Cross-side and same-side effects; positive vs. negative network effects (congestion, noise)
Critical mass, winner-take-all dynamics, and when multi-platform markets survive
Evaluating whether a startup idea can realistically build network effects in today's market

## Takeaway
Network effect audit — map your platform's effects by type, score strength, identify the single fragility point
