## Understanding Systems Thinking
Systems thinking recognizes the complexity of issues and actions in the real world. While teh idea of domino effect can help us understand how one change or actions can trigger a chain reaction leading to a set of changes or actions elsewhere, the idea of a casecase effect accounts ofr compelxities associated with unpredicability. uncertainty and the potential for system-wide collapse.

A particularly helpful method ot better understand systems thinking int eh contex tof business sustainability is through building blocks or systems Jenga exercises. Such as exercise is highly adaptable and has proven to eb effective for teaching a wider range of audiences about systems thinking, including corporate executive and board members.
