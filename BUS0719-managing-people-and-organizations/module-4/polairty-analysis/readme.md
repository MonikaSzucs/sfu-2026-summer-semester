# Polarity Analysis

After instruction on the nature of polarities and their centrality to core dilemmas in organizations and change, you will analyze how an organization you have worked in managed a polarity and develop a more robust understanding of that polarity.

You are to examine one polarity in any organization you have worked in, past or present, that was not managed well. A polarity is an interdependent pair (neither can exist without the other) that appears to make opposing demands on an organization. There are two parts to the assignment. The first is to analyze how this polarity operated within that organization and the negative effects of its management. The second is to propose how the organization could have better managed this polarity.

You must choose one of the polarities on the Polarity Analysis document, or contact Dr. Bushe if you want to explore a different polarity.
