# Module 1: Platform Fundamentals — Pipeline vs. Platform Thinking

## Topic 
- How platforms differ from pipeline businesses: the shift from value creation to value facilitation
- The four participant types — owners, producers, consumers, partners — and how each captures value differently
- Debate 'what is actually a platform?': use ambiguous real cases — Shopify, Claude, Clio, Substack — to stress-test the definition
- Platform types across industries: transaction, innovation, integration — where does your business fit?
- Why the world's most valuable companies (Meta, Alphabet, Amazon, Apple, Microsoft) are all platform businesses

## Takeaway
Platform Canvas — Map your own business or startup idea as Pipeline vs. Platform
