# Module 5: Monetization – Pricing Strategy For Platforms 

## Topic 
Who to charge, who to subsidize — the pricing asymmetry principle and why getting it backwards is fatal 
Five monetization model types: transaction fee, subscription, freemium, data monetization, enhanced access 
When free builds moats vs. when free destroys value — the LinkedIn freemium case 
Extracting value without killing the network: the governance balance (Keurig 2.0 as cautionary tale) 
* Platform tax and pricing power: what startups give up when they monetize on top of someone else's platform 

## Takeaway
Monetization architecture doc — one page showing which side pays, what they pay for, and when
